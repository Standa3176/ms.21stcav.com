# Publishing must resolve the price, not forward the placeholder

## Problem

Auto-created products went LIVE carrying the supplier's RRP. On 2026-08-28, **23 of 23**
products in one batch were mispriced; the worst was `4807101` (Fellowes 23" privacy filter)
live at **£126.54** against a £25.16 cost, a 35% rule that implies £40.76, and a stable
competitor at £34.26. Correct price: £34.25. **3.7× over market.**

## Cause

`GenerateProductDraftsCommand:252` seeds `sell_price` from the supplier feed's `rrp`
(falling back to `buy_price × 1.4`) so a draft is never NULL. Its own comment states the
intent: *"PriceCalculator tier rules can refine later"*. Nothing refined it before publish.

`PublishProductJob::buildCreatePayload()` forwarded `$product->sell_price` verbatim as
`regular_price`. It already had `PriceCalculator` injected — but used it only for `stripVat`.

**The guard already existed in the sibling writer.** `CreateWooProductJob::computeSellPennies()`
resolves the rule and computes the price before its POST. Two product-creating writers, one
priced correctly and one did not.

RRP is not merely approximate, it is **arbitrary**: the same MPN carries £36.00, £233.00 and
£126.54 across three `feeds_products` rows, and the winner is whichever supplier uploaded last.

## Blast radius

`routes/console.php:766-780` schedules `products:draft-from-suggestions --auto-approve`
**Mon+Thu 04:00 UTC straight to live**, capped at 25. `WOO_WRITE_ENABLED=true` since cutover,
so the docblock's "safe shadow no-op" no longer applies. The corrective
`pricing:undercut-competitors --live` runs 07:00 daily — so the exposure was a **3-hour
window, twice weekly, up to 25 products**, plus anything that run skips (it skips null/zero
`buy_price`, which is exactly when RRP was the only anchor).

## Fix

In `buildCreatePayload`, for auto-created rows with a real cost, derive `regular_price` via
`RuleResolver` + `PriceCalculator` — the same call `CreateWooProductJob` makes — falling back
to the stored `sell_price` otherwise.

Deliberate boundaries:

- **`auto_create_status === 'manual'` is untouched.** An operator price is a decision, not a
  placeholder.
- **`NoPricingRuleMatchedException` falls back rather than failing.** `CreateWooProductJob`
  lets it propagate to its retry chain, which is right *there* — a draft nobody can price
  should not be created. Here the product exists and an operator asked for it to go live;
  refusing would regress a path that previously always succeeded. Only three rules exist, all
  `default_tier`, so a cost outside every tier range is a live possibility.
- **`RuleResolver`/`PriceCalculator` are reused, not reimplemented.** They are byte-locked by
  `TradePricingNoV1ModificationTest`; duplicating the arithmetic would be the wrong move.

## Not fixed here

Competitor undercutting still happens only in the 07:00 run, so a product goes live at
cost-plus and moves to the competitor price later. Cost-plus is a defensible price to sit at
for a few hours. RRP is not.

## Tests

Three new cases in `PublishProductJobTest`:

1. a draft carrying £126.54 RRP on a £25.16 cost publishes at the 22% rule price (£36.83)
2. a `manual` row publishes at its stored £99.00 untouched
3. zero `buy_price` falls back to the stored value rather than blocking the publish

`handle()` gained a 6th injected parameter, so 17 existing call sites across
`PublishProductJobTest` and `PublishProductGlobalAttributesTest` were updated.
